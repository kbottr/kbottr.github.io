## The problem

Drivers didn't trust the map. Chargers shown as available were often broken or occupied, so people planned around the app instead of with it.

## The approach

We rebuilt the map around live reliability data and a single "will it work?" signal, then tested three densities of information with 24 drivers.

## The outcome

Failed charging attempts reported in support tickets dropped by a third within two months of launch.
